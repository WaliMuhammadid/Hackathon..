# 🎬 Practice Phone — 2-Minute Pitch & Demo Script
**Event:** Build4Better Hackathon (NED University)  
**Target Time:** 1:55 - 2:00 minutes total

---

### [0:00 - 0:15] 1. The Problem (15 seconds)
> **Visual:** Close-up of an elderly hand hesitating over a smartphone touchscreen, showing an intimidating SMS scam and confusing banking menu.
>
> **Voiceover (Urdu/English):**  
> *"In Pakistan, our parents and grandparents want to stay connected with family and manage everyday tasks independently. But fear holds them back — fear of losing money, breaking their phone, or falling for lottery and banking scams. What they need is a safe, guided sandbox to practice without consequences."*

---

### [0:15 - 0:50] 2. The Solution & Meezan Safe Transfer Flow (35 seconds)
> **Visual:** The camera cuts to Practice Phone on screen (390x844 responsive mobile frame). Switch language to **اردو**. The user taps Meezan Bank.
>
> **Voiceover:**  
> *"Meet **Practice Phone** — an interactive simulator featuring real Pakistani apps like Meezan Bank, WhatsApp, and SMS, powered by an AI Coach with visual spotlight and bilingual voice guidance."*
>
> **Demo Action:**  
> - Coach highlights eye toggle: user unhides balance with spoken Urdu audio: *"بیلنس دیکھنے کے لیے آنکھ کے نشان پر دبائیں"*.
> - User taps **Send Money** -> Selects **Bilal Bhai** -> Enters **PKR 1000** -> Proceeds.
> - An SMS notification drops from the top containing a 6-digit OTP (`482910`).
> - Coach warns in red: *"Never share this OTP with anyone!"*
> - User enters OTP -> Transfer success receipt generates instantly.

---

### [0:50 - 1:25] 3. Scam Shield & Video Call Troubleshooting (35 seconds)
> **Visual:** Home screen -> Launch **SMS & Safety** app.
>
> **Demo Action:**  
> - User opens the urgent message: *"MUBARAK! Aapka Benazir Kafalat Program me 50,000 ka inam nikla hai..."*
> - User taps the big red **"Scam"** button.
> - An instant breakdown displays red flags: unknown mobile number, urgency, fake `.xyz` link.
> - User taps **"Block & Report"**.
> - Switch to **Video Call**: Simulated internet failure triggers *"Can't connect? Helper Checklist"*. User turns Wi-Fi ON with one tap and connects immediately to Ammi.

---

### [1:25 - 1:50] 4. Progress, Urdu Narration & Learning Modes (25 seconds)
> **Visual:** Open **"My Progress"** modal and switch between **Guided**, **Try**, and **Test** modes.
>
> **Voiceover:**  
> *"Practice Phone accommodates every learning speed. In **Guided Mode**, the coach provides continuous voice and visual cues. In **Try Mode**, users test themselves with on-demand hints. In **Test Mode**, mistakes are tallied into a 3-star rating, unlocking badges like 'Scam Spotter' and 'Digital Banker' saved locally."*

---

### [1:50 - 2:00] 5. Honest Disclosure & Closing (10 seconds)
> **Visual:** Showing the **Practice Mode** watermark and GitHub repo.
>
> **Voiceover:**  
> *"Everything you see is 100% simulated client-side — no real accounts or funds are ever touched. Practice Phone empowers Pakistan's elders with confidence, digital literacy, and safety. Built for Build4Better at NED University. Thank you!"*
